## Strict Rule & Architecture Policy: Premium Custom Animated Emojis

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **RULE: ALL emojis displayed in customer-facing Telegram Bot messages or inline buttons MUST strictly use Telegram Premium animated custom emojis.**
> - Use `tgEmoji(BOT_EMOJIS.<NAME>, '<fallback>')` or `<tg-emoji emoji-id="...">` for all emojis in message texts (headings, bullet points, statistics, warnings, payment options, instructions).
> - Use `icon_custom_emoji_id: BOT_EMOJIS.<NAME>` on all inline keyboard buttons.
> - **NEVER** leave raw Unicode emojis (e.g. `👤`, `💳`, `🛍`, `📦`, `💰`, `⚠️`, `✨`) in message templates.

## Architecture Evolution: Admin Dashboard "Orders & Payments" Split

Due to the transition to the **Prepaid Wallet Architecture (Recommendations A to D)**:
1. **Product Purchases** are 100% instant and paid via Wallet balance. Digital credentials are sent to customer in 0 seconds.
2. **Payment Slips** are now strictly for **Wallet Top-Up / Deposits** (`TOP-XXXXXX`).

### Recommended Admin Navigation & Structure:
`ORDERS & PAYMENTS`
- **`🛍 Product Orders` (`/orders`):**
  - Displays instant digital product purchases.
  - Payment method: Wallet Balance (`MMK`).
  - Status: `DELIVERED`, `PROCESSING_SUPPLIER`, `CANCELLED`.
  - Credentials viewer modal with re-send / refund option.
- **`💳 Deposit Slips` (`/payments`):**
  - Displays customer KBZPay / WavePay screenshot slips for Wallet Top-Up.
  - Shows `Review` badge with pending count.
  - 1-Click `Approve` credits the customer's wallet and triggers automated Telegram Bot celebration message.
